Product FAQ

Questions About Custom Service 2 Steps Detox Foot Spa Pedicure Kit,Salon Manicure Pedicure Set

What is a Foot Exfoliating Crystal Jelly Spa Kit?

Foot Exfoliating Crystal Jelly Spa Kit is a 2-step foot care product that combines a jelly soak packet and a salt scrub packet. The jelly helps soften rough skin during soaking, while the salt packet helps gently exfoliate dead skin and leave feet feeling smoother and refreshed.

How do you use the 2-step crystal jelly foot spa?

First, dissolve the jelly packet in warm water and soak feet for about 10–15 minutes. Then add the salt packet and gently massage the feet to exfoliate rough or dry skin. After use, rinse feet with clean water and dry thoroughly.

What are the main benefits of crystal jelly foot spa treatment?

The main benefits include softening rough feet, helping remove dead skin, smoothing dry heels, refreshing tired feet, and creating a spa-like foot care experience at home, in salons, or for private label foot care brands.

Is this foot spa kit suitable for private label or OEM foot care brands?

Yes. This type of 2-step foot spa kit is suitable for private label, OEM and ODM foot care product lines. The formula, scent, packaging, logo, color, sachet design and product weight can usually be customized for beauty brands, salons, spas and e-commerce sellers.

What scent options are available for the crystal jelly foot spa?

Common scent options include rose, lavender and mint. Rose is ideal for a soft floral spa experience, lavender is suitable for relaxing foot care, and mint gives a refreshing feeling for tired feet. Custom fragrances can also be developed for private label orders.
