Product FAQ

Questions About Quick-Absorbing Bronzing Oil for Indoor & Outdoor Tanning

What is this tanning oil designed for?

It is designed to complement indoor and outdoor tanning routines while moisturizing the skin and helping create a radiant, sun-kissed appearance.

Can it be used for both indoor and outdoor tanning?

Yes. It can be applied before outdoor sunbathing or as part of an indoor tanning-bed routine.

What are the key oils in the formula?

The featured ingredients include olive fruit oil, carrot seed oil, coconut oil, jojoba seed oil, squalane and vitamin E.

Does the tanning oil feel greasy?

It is designed with a lightweight, quick-absorbing texture that leaves the skin feeling smooth and nourished without a heavy, greasy feel.

Can the product be customized for private label brands?

Yes. OEM/ODM options can include custom branding, formula, fragrance, color, packaging and product size.
