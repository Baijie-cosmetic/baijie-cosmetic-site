Product FAQ

Questions About Natural-looking Tanning Serum Drops to Add to Moisturizer for Face & Body

What is a face and neck tanning serum used for?

A face and neck tanning serum is used to help build a natural-looking, sun-kissed glow on the face and neck. It is usually lightweight, easy to layer, and designed for a smoother, more even tanning routine.

How do you use Extreme Tan Face & Neck Tanning Serum?

Apply 2–3 drops onto clean skin, then smooth evenly over the face and neck. Gently massage until fully absorbed, then layer it into your regular tanning or skincare routine.

Can this tanning serum be used with a tanning bed?

Yes. This tanning serum can be used as part of an indoor tanning bed routine or outdoor sunbathing routine. Follow the product instructions and always use sun care products as needed.

Is this tanning serum suitable for daily use?

Yes. The lightweight, fast-absorbing serum texture is suitable for daily use to help gradually build a natural-looking glow. Adjust usage based on your skin tone, routine, and desired bronze effect.

Does this face tanning serum feel heavy or sticky?

No. The formula is designed to feel lightweight and comfortable on the skin. It absorbs quickly and layers easily with other skincare or tanning products.
