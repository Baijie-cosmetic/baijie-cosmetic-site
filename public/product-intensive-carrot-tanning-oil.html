Product FAQ

Questions About Intensive Carrot Tanning Oil

What is organic tanning oil used for?

Organic tanning oil is used as part of a body tanning routine to help support a natural sun-kissed or golden bronze appearance. This 200ml tanning oil is designed for beach days, summer skincare routines, vacations, and customers who want a smooth radiant glow.

Is this tanning oil greasy on the skin?

This tanning oil is positioned as a lightweight, non-greasy body oil. It is designed to spread easily and leave the skin feeling smooth and moisturized without a heavy oily finish.

Who is this 200ml tanning oil suitable for?

This 200ml tanning oil is suitable for users who want an easy-to-apply body tanning oil for beach days, poolside use, outdoor summer routines, or daily glow care. It is also suitable for beauty brands looking for a private label tanning product.

How should tanning oil be applied for an even-looking finish?

For a smoother-looking finish, apply the tanning oil evenly to clean, dry skin and massage it over the body in circular motions. Even application helps avoid streaks or uneven marks and supports a more consistent bronze glow.

Can this tanning oil be customized for private label brands?

Yes. This type of tanning oil can be customized with a private label logo, bottle color, label design, fruit-inspired scent concept, packaging box, capacity options, and brand positioning. It is suitable for OEM/ODM tanning brands, cosmetic suppliers, and wholesale beauty projects.
