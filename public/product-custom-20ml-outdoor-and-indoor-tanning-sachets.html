Product FAQ

Questions About Custom 20ml Outdoor & Indoor Tanning Sachets

What are tanning lotion sachets used for?

Tanning lotion sachets are single-use packets designed for convenient tanning care. They can be used as part of indoor tanning, outdoor tanning, beach beauty routines, salon sample programs, travel kits, and private label tanning product lines.

Are 20ml tanning sachets good for travel?

Yes. A 20ml tanning sachet is lightweight, compact, and easy to pack in a handbag, beach bag, gym bag, or travel kit. The single-use format makes it practical for vacations, tanning salons, hotels, and on-the-go beauty routines.

What makes tanning lotion sachets suitable for private label brands?

Tanning lotion sachets are highly flexible for private label brands because the sachet artwork, logo, formula concept, fragrance, tanning level, ingredient positioning, and outer packaging can be customized. They are also suitable for sample packs, trial kits, and wholesale promotional sets.

Can tanning sachets be used for both indoor and outdoor tanning?

These tanning sachets can be positioned for indoor and outdoor tanning routines, depending on the final formula and brand instructions. They are suitable for customers who want an easy-to-apply tanning lotion in a portable single-use format.

What ingredients can be highlighted in tanning lotion sachets?

Common ingredient-focused positioning for tanning lotion sachets includes aloe vera, vitamin E, tyrosine, coconut oil, and moisturizing botanical ingredients. These ingredients help support hydration, comfort, and a smooth tanning routine without making medical or exaggerated claims.
