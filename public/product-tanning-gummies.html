Product FAQ

Questions About Tanning Gummies

What are tanning gummies used for?

Tanning gummies are beauty supplement-style gummies designed for people who want to support a healthy-looking, sun-kissed glow as part of their daily beauty routine. This watermelon tanning gummies product is positioned for glow-focused, summer lifestyle, and beauty-from-within product lines.

How many gummies are in one bottle?

Each bottle contains 60 tanning gummies. The product can be positioned as an easy daily beauty habit and is suitable for customers who prefer a convenient gummy format over drops, sprays, lotions, or capsules.

Are tanning gummies suitable for a sun-free glow routine?

Yes. These tanning gummies are marketed for customers who want a sun-free glow routine and a radiant, healthy-looking appearance. For compliant product listings, avoid promising permanent tanning results and describe the product as supporting a sun-kissed look or glow-focused beauty routine.

Who are tanning gummies suitable for?

Watermelon tanning gummies are suitable for beauty consumers who want an easy, flavorful, and portable glow product. They are especially suitable for summer beauty shoppers, beach lifestyle collections, TikTok Shop products, Shopify stores, Amazon sellers, and private label beauty supplement brands.

Can tanning gummies be customized for private label brands?

Yes. This tanning gummies product can be customized for private label projects, including custom logo, bottle label, flavor, gummy shape, bottle color, packaging box, ingredient positioning, and product claims. It is suitable for OEM/ODM beauty supplement brands, tanning product suppliers, and wholesale beauty businesses.
