Deeply Hydrate Soothing Gentle Exfoliation & Pore Care PDRN Facial Toner Pads

Serum-soaked PDRN facial toner pads for deep hydration, soothing care, gentle exfoliation and pore care. The cooling essence helps relieve the feeling of overheated skin, while soft embossed pads hold essence and sit comfortably against the skin. Sodium DNA (low-molecular-weight PDRN), panthenol, bakuchiol and hydrolyzed marine collagen support the moisture barrier and leave skin looking smooth, plump and radiant. Use as part of daily skincare or to prep a smoother-looking base for makeup.

What size are the PDRN facial toner pads?
