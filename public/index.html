FAQ

OEM / ODM Questions Buyers Ask Most

What is your MOQ?

MOQ depends on formula and packaging. We support flexible MOQ for selected ready formulas and higher MOQ for fully customized projects.

Can I use my own logo and packaging design?

Yes. We support private label logos, label design, bottle selection, tube packaging, jars, boxes and complete packaging customization.

Can you customize formula, scent and texture?

Yes. Our team can adjust formula direction, fragrance, texture, color, active ingredients and product positioning based on your market.

How long does sample development take?

Most sample projects take about 7–15 working days after confirming the formula direction and packaging requirements.

Do you support global shipping?

Yes. We support export packing and global delivery for distributors, ecommerce sellers, salon brands and beauty startups.

Can you help with documents such as MSDS?

Yes. We can support basic export documents, product specifications and safety documents depending on the product and destination market.
