Product FAQ

Questions About Extreme Tan Accelerator Stick

What is a tanning stick used for?

A tanning stick is used as part of a tanning routine to help support a bronzed, sun-kissed look. This solid balm format is easy to apply directly to the skin and is suitable for customers who prefer a portable, mess-free tanning product.

Is this tanning wand suitable for indoor and outdoor tanning?

Yes. This Tanning Wand is positioned for both indoor and outdoor tanning routines. It can be used for beach days, poolside beauty care, vacations, and daily body glow routines where users want a smooth, bronzed appearance.

What makes a tanning stick different from tanning oil or lotion?

A tanning stick has a solid balm texture that can be applied directly to the skin without spilling or dripping. Compared with oils or lotions, it is more portable, travel-friendly, and convenient for targeted body application.

Does this tanning stick feel moisturizing on the skin?

Yes. The product concept highlights a creamy balm texture with moisturizing ingredients such as seed butter, walnut seed oil, carrot seed oil, and vitamin E. It is designed to leave skin feeling smooth, nourished, and comfortable.

Can this tanning stick be customized for private label brands?

Yes. This 100g tanning stick is suitable for private label customization, including custom logo, label artwork, scent options, ingredient positioning, packaging design, and OEM/ODM tanning product development for beauty brands and wholesalers.
