FAQ

What cosmetic products can you manufacture?

We can manufacture lip gloss, lip balm, foundation, mascara, blush, highlighter and other cosmetic products depending on your project requirements.

Can you customize colors and shades?

Yes. We support color matching, texture adjustment, finish selection and packaging customization for private label cosmetic projects.

Do you provide OEM and ODM services?

Yes. OEM is available when you already have formula or specifications. ODM is available when you need our team to help develop the product concept and formula.

How do I start a cosmetic project?

Send us your product type, target market, quantity, packaging idea and formula requirements. Our team will recommend a suitable solution and sample plan.
