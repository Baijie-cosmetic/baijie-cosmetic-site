Product FAQ

Questions About Tanning Gel and Cream

What is tanning cream and tanning gel used for?

Tanning cream and gel are used as part of a body tanning routine to help support a natural sun-kissed glow. This type of product is popular for summer skincare, beach holidays, and customers who want a smooth cream texture with a fresh fruit-inspired scent.

What is the difference between tanning cream and tanning gel?

Tanning cream usually has a richer, moisturizing texture that spreads smoothly on the skin, while tanning gel often feels lighter, silkier, and more refreshing. Both formats can be used in tanning care routines, but the best choice depends on the customer’s preferred texture and product experience.

Who is intensive tanning gel suitable for?

Intensive tanning gel is suitable for customers who want a deeper-looking bronze glow and an easy-to-apply body tanning product. It is especially suitable for beach lovers, summer beauty shoppers, vacation users, and people who prefer a hydrating gel texture.

Why are fruit-scented tanning products popular for e-commerce?

Fruit-scented tanning products are popular because they combine strong visual appeal, summer lifestyle positioning, and a pleasant beauty routine experience. Peach and watermelon scent concepts are easy to market on Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop, Alibaba, and private label beauty websites.

Can tanning cream and tanning gel be customized for private label brands?

Yes. Tanning cream and tanning gel can be customized for private label brands with custom logo, label design, jar or bottle packaging, scent options, texture, capacity, and outer box design. These products are suitable for OEM/ODM tanning product lines, beauty suppliers, and wholesale cosmetic projects.
