Product FAQ

Questions About Instant Bronze SPF Shimmering Body Tanning Oil Spray

What is glitter tanning oil used for?

Glitter tanning oil is used to give the skin a radiant, sun-kissed glow with a shimmering finish. It is commonly applied to the body, arms, shoulders, legs, and collarbone area for beach days, vacations, parties, photoshoots, and summer beauty routines.

Is shimmer mist tanning oil easy to apply?

Yes. This shimmer mist tanning oil comes in a spray bottle, making it easy to apply quickly and evenly over the body. The mist format is convenient for users who want a fast glow routine without a heavy or complicated application process.

What shades are available for this glitter tanning oil?

This glitter tanning oil is available in four glow shades: Baby Pink, Golden Beige, Warm Golden, and Deep Bronze. These shade options help users choose a soft highlight, warm glow, or deeper bronzed finish depending on their skin tone and style preference.

Who is shimmer tanning oil suitable for?

Shimmer tanning oil is suitable for customers who want a glowing, dewy, and bronzed body look. It is especially suitable for beachgoers, vacation travelers, festival looks, summer skincare routines, body makeup users, and beauty consumers who like luminous skin finishes.

Can glitter tanning oil be customized for private label brands?

Yes. Glitter tanning oil is suitable for private label customization, including custom logo, bottle design, label artwork, fragrance, shade range, packaging box, and formula positioning. It is a strong product option for tanning brands, body care brands, Shopify stores, Amazon sellers, and OEM/ODM cosmetic projects.
