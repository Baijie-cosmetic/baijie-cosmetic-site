Product FAQ

Questions About Body Adhesive Glue for Clothes to Skin, Sweat Proof, Wash Off

What can this body glue be used for?

It is designed for multiple styling applications, including clothing, bra straps, wigs, stagewear, cosplay pieces, socks, stockings and accessories.

Does the body glue dry clear?

Yes. The product is designed with a clear formula that dries transparent for a discreet, seamless-looking finish.

How do I use the body glue?

Apply a small amount to clean, dry, oil-free skin. Wait about 20 seconds until tacky, then press the item into place for approximately 10 seconds.

How do I remove it?

The glue can be washed off with soap and water.

Can I customize this product for my own brand?

Yes. OEM/ODM and private label options can include custom logo, packaging, tube color, size and other brand-specific requirements.
