Product FAQ

Questions About Deep Bronzer Intensive Tanning Butter

What is instant tan butter used for?

Instant tan butter is used to give the skin an immediate bronze glow without waiting for a gradual tan to develop. This 100ml tanning cream is suitable for users who want a quick natural-looking finish for daily beauty routines, events, beach days, or summer skin styling.

Is this tanning butter suitable for a natural-looking bronze finish?

Yes. This tanning butter is designed to support a smooth, natural-looking bronze glow. Its key positioning focuses on no orange tones, streak-free coverage, and a lightweight texture that helps the color look more even when applied properly.

Does this instant tan butter contain DHA?

This product can be positioned as a no-DHA, wash-off tanning butter. It is suitable for users who prefer instant cosmetic color rather than a DHA-based self-tanner that develops over several hours.

How do you apply tanning butter evenly?

For best results, apply tanning butter to clean, dry skin and blend evenly using circular motions or a tanning mitt. Pay extra attention to elbows, knees, ankles, and wrists to help avoid streaks or uneven marks.

Can this tanning butter be customized for private label beauty brands?

Yes. This type of tanning butter is suitable for private label customization, including custom logo printing, tube packaging design, shade selection, fragrance options, ingredient positioning, and OEM/ODM tanning product development for beauty brands and wholesale suppliers.
