Questions About Private Label 4 Steps Foot Care Set Collagen Crystal Spa Pedicure Kit

What is a 4 in 1 Foot Care Set?

in 1 Foot Care Set is a complete foot spa kit that includes foot soak, salt scrub, mud mask and foot lotion. It is designed to help soften tired feet, exfoliate rough skin, cleanse impurities and nourish the skin for a smoother foot care experience.

How do you use a 4-step foot spa kit?

Use the kit in four steps: first soak feet with the foot soak, then massage with the salt scrub to exfoliate dead skin, apply the mud mask to cleanse and refresh the skin, and finish with foot lotion to moisturize and soften the feet.

What are the benefits of foot care set?

The foot care set helps create a relaxing spa-like foot care experience. It can soften rough feet, smooth dry skin, refresh tired feet and leave the skin feeling hydrated.

Is this foot spa set suitable for private label or OEM brands?

Yes. This 4 in 1 foot care set is suitable for private label, OEM and ODM foot care brands. The formula, fragrance, sachet design, box packaging, logo, color theme and product weight can be customized for beauty brands, spas, salons, gift sets and e-commerce sellers.

What products are included in the 4 in 1 foot care set?

The set usually includes four single-use sachets: foot soak, salt scrub, foot mud mask and foot lotion. Each step works together to cleanse, exfoliate, smooth and nourish the feet, making it suitable for at-home foot spa routines and professional foot care treatments.
